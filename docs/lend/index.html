Lend your PC

A friend is lending you their PC.

This link has no words in it.

Open hivedesk on your computer, choose Use a friend's PC and enter these six words (or paste this link into the first box).

Or start hivedesk with the link and the window fills itself:

$ npx hivedesk --join "..."

Ask your friend to run npx hivedesk --lend and send you the link or the six words it shows.

  • Nothing is sent from this page. The words live in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to a server.
  • The words work once and expire after 10 minutes.
  • Only model prompts and answers travel between the two hivedesks. Your friend's files are never read or written, and they can stop lending at any time.
