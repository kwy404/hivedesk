hivedesk @VERSION@ for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel)
Your agents. Your machines. Your City.

INSTALL
  Drag hivedesk to Applications, then open it from Applications.

FIRST LAUNCH
  hivedesk is signed ad hoc, not with an Apple Developer ID, and it is not notarized,
  so macOS asks once:
  1. Open hivedesk. macOS says it cannot verify the app. Click Done (or Cancel).
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security.
  3. Scroll down. Next to "hivedesk was blocked", click Open Anyway, then confirm
     with your password or Touch ID.
  4. Open hivedesk again. macOS does not ask again.
  On older macOS versions: Control click hivedesk in Applications and choose Open.
  In a terminal, this does the same:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/hivedesk.app

UPDATES
  hivedesk checks for a new version when it starts and every 6 hours. Updates are signed
  (Ed25519) and checked with SHA-256 before they replace the app; if a new version does not
  start, the previous one comes back. Turn it off in the menu: Update automatically.

PRIMEIRA ABERTURA (pt-BR)
  Arraste o hivedesk para Aplicativos e abra. Se o macOS disser que não pode verificar o app,
  abra Ajustes do Sistema, Privacidade e Segurança, clique em Abrir Mesmo Assim ao lado do
  hivedesk e confirme. Depois disso ele abre normalmente.
